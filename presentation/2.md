# Problems Tables Memo Solves

## 1. Grammar Is Learnt in Isolation, Not as a System

Most language apps (Duolingo, Anki, etc.) teach individual words or sentences. When a learner encounters a conjugation like *habláis*, they may recognize it — but they cannot confidently produce *corréis* or *saltáis* because they never internalized the **pattern**, only the specific word.

**Tables Memo** presents grammar as a 2D table: pronouns × verbs. Completing the whole table for multiple verbs simultaneously forces the learner to see and internalize the pattern rather than individual forms.

---

## 2. No Active Recall of Word Forms in Context

Passive recognition (reading flashcards) is far weaker than **production** (typing or constructing the form yourself). Most tools default to recognition.

Tables Memo uses exercise modes that require the learner to:
- Reconstruct a word form by applying insertion/deletion operations step by step
- Place a form into a real sentence with a blank
- Type or select forms without seeing the answer first

---

## 3. Vocabulary and Grammar Are Treated as Separate Subjects

In reality, every word is a grammar object: a verb belongs to a conjugation class; a noun belongs to a declension class. Learning *comer* means nothing if you cannot use *como / comes / come* correctly.

Tables Memo links **vocabulary words** to **grammar rule tables**, so every new word immediately comes with its full inflection set and example sentences — grammar and vocabulary are learned together.

---

## 4. Example Sentences Are Invented Per Word, Which Doesn't Scale

Language apps typically store one hard-coded sentence per word. As the vocabulary grows, sentence quality degrades and coverage becomes inconsistent.

Tables Memo uses a **sentence template** approach: a sentence like  
*"Yo _____ en la casa todos los días."*  
is a reusable template that any AR-verb in the first-person row can fill. Templates are categorized by word category and optionally by grammar rule, so the system can match the right sentence to each word form automatically. New words get high-quality sentences without writing one from scratch for each word.

---

## 5. Adding New Content Is Manual and Expensive

In a conventional app, adding the verb *bailar* requires:
- Writing all 6 conjugated forms manually
- Writing 6 example sentences manually
- Categorizing the word manually

Tables Memo is designed for LLM-assisted content generation: when a base word is added, the system determines which grammar rule tables it belongs to, fills in all cell values, and maps or generates sentences — reducing per-word authoring cost to near zero while keeping the human in control for review.

---

## 6. Lack of Structural Progress Tracking

Knowing "I've reviewed 300 words" is not meaningful. The more useful question is: *"Which conjugation cells for which verbs have I mastered?"*

Tables Memo tracks correctness **per word form** (per cell), so a learner can see precisely which pronoun–verb combinations need more practice rather than reviewing the whole table again.
